## Explanation
Weathering breaks down or alters rock at its location. Physical weathering fragments it; chemical weathering changes its minerals. Movement of the resulting material is a separate process.

## Example
Water freezing inside a crack can widen it; iron-bearing minerals can oxidize. The first example is physical fragmentation, the second chemical change.

## Common mistake
Breaking rock and moving the fragments are distinct. Rock can weather without being carried away.
