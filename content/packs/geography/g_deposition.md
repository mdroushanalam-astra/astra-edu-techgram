## Explanation
Deposition occurs when transported material settles or is left behind. A reduction in transport capacity can encourage deposition, but settling also depends on particle size, turbulence and the environment. Not every river mouth forms a delta.

## Example
A simplified classroom model slows sediment-bearing water and lets some particles settle. Real river mouths also involve waves, tides, sediment supply and basin shape.

## Common mistake
Avoid the rule that slower water always deposits every particle immediately. Fine material can remain suspended.
