## Explanation
Movement and deformation inside Earth can uplift, fold or fault the crust. Weathering and erosion act on exposed material; they are different processes from the internal forces that build relief.

## Example
Imagine a stack of soft layers pushed from its sides: it may fold. This is an analogy for compression, not a complete physical model of Earth's crust.

## Common mistake
Do not attribute every mountain or fault to flowing water. Separate internal deformation from surface processes.
