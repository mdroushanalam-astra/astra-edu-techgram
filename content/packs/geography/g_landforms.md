## Explanation
River landforms reflect erosion, transport and deposition over time. A channel can cut a valley, shifting channels can shape floodplains, and sediment accumulation can build a delta where local conditions allow it.

## Example
A river can erode one bank while sediment accumulates elsewhere. Read a landform as evidence of processes, not proof of one process acting alone.

## Common mistake
A fertile floodplain is not necessarily safe from flooding, and a river mouth does not necessarily have a delta.
