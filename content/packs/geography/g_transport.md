## Explanation
Transportation is the movement of sediment after it is entrained. Rivers move material in suspension, along the bed, and in solution. The amount and kind of load depend on flow, sediment properties and other conditions.

## Example
Compare a particle suspended in flowing water with a pebble rolling along the bed. Both move, but they do not use the same transport mechanism.

## Common mistake
Transportation describes movement. Deposition describes material settling out or being left behind; they can occur in different parts of one reach.
