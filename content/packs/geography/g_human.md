## Explanation
River plains can offer fertile soil, water and transport opportunities. Settlement decisions must also consider flooding, channel movement and other hazards. Benefits and risks can coexist at the same site.

## Example
Compare two building sites: one near water on a floodplain and another on higher ground. Evaluate access and exposure rather than choosing solely by soil fertility.

## Common mistake
Present-day water level alone cannot establish long-term flood safety.

## Hint 1
Identify the process being asked about before choosing an option.

## Hint 2
Compare the options by whether material changes, moves, or settles. Use the definition for this concept.
