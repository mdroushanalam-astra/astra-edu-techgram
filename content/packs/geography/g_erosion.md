## Explanation
Erosion removes material from a surface. Water, wind, ice and gravity can contribute, with their relative importance varying by landscape and conditions. Weathering and erosion can operate together.

## Example
A stream picking up loose particles from its bank removes them. The particles being carried downstream are then undergoing transportation.

## Common mistake
An agent is not universally dominant in every desert or every river. The setting and conditions matter.
