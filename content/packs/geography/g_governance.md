## Explanation
Flood planning combines observations of channels, floodplains and past events with appropriate hydraulic analysis and local evidence. Infrastructure changes flow patterns and can transfer risk; effects depend on design and context.

## Example
Before proposing an embankment, identify who and what it protects, where water may be redirected, and how the proposal will be assessed. This lesson is a conceptual exercise, not an engineering design.

## Common mistake
A single structure does not eliminate every flood hazard. Avoid presenting a simplified diagram as a site-specific prediction.

## Hint 1
Identify the process being asked about before choosing an option.

## Hint 2
Compare the options by whether material changes, moves, or settles. Use the definition for this concept.
